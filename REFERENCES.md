# References

The sources the harness's question set is derived from. Every detail below was
taken from the documents themselves, not from memory.

**The plugin ships none of these texts.** It encodes its own question set,
modelled on them, and points from each canvas element to its source. Readers who
hold a local copy point `PLACET_RESEARCH_LIBRARY` at it; readers who do not get
the citation.

## The canvas

Dorobantu, S., Gruber, M., Ravasi, D., & Wellman, N. (2024). The AMJ Management
Research Canvas: A Tool for Conducting and Reporting Empirical Research.
*Academy of Management Journal, 67*(5), 1163–1174.
https://doi.org/10.5465/amj.2024.4005

Nine elements in four areas: Puzzle · Audience and Prior Research · Research
Question (framing); Theoretical Constructs and Relationships (theoretical
framework); Research Setting · Research Design and Analysis (empirical
methodology); Findings · Contributions · Boundary Conditions and Limitations
(conclusions). The worksheet is Table 1.

article header identifies it as 2011, Vol. 54, No. 4. The article header governs.

## Problematization

Monteiro, E., Constantinides, P., Scott, S., Shaikh, M., & Burton-Jones, A.
(2022). Qualitative Research Methods in Information Systems: A Call for
Phenomenon-Focused Problematization. *MIS Quarterly, 46*(4), iii–xix.
(Editor's Comments)

Four sensibilities — enter a dialogue with the empirical setting; challenge
assumptions; ask how we got here; make the invisible visible — crossed with three
lines of inquiry (*what / when / where*) in Table 2.
