# 1 · Puzzle

## 1.1 What is the broad question this project speaks to?

Senior developers who use an AI assistant every day do not hand it the whole task: they bring it in at some steps and keep others. What decides which steps go to the assistant is not clear from the accounts we have. [proposed, src: README.md, src: Interview_P05.md]

# 7 · Findings

## 7.1 What does the material show — separated from what you make of it?

One participant wrote the first version of a task alone and brought the assistant in when the requirements grew (P05). One feeds the findings of a code-quality tool to it, one by one (P09). One asks it for unit tests for new functionality and checks them, and for documentation when the quality tool complains (P10).
